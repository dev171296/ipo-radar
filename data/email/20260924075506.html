NVIDIA — in its own words (moonshotai/kimi-k3)

On listing (38). Subscription stood at only ~0.33x on the morning of the final day, which historically signals a weak debut unless a last-day QIB surge materialises. The price is also being asked at ~48x earnings, above the document's own stated peer P/Es of 8.5–30.5, leaving little margin of safety for flip buyers.

On the long term (42). Revenue has grown just 2.65% over two years [business, page 212] and PAT margins hover around 2–4%, which is commodity-tile economics, not a compounding story. Positives are improving cash conversion, a current ratio near 1.7 and deleveraging (debt-to-equity down from 0.68 to 0.44), but at 48x earnings the price already pays for growth the numbers do not show.

On governance (45). Promoter shares are pledged to 360 ONE Prime as security for a loan to a promoter-linked entity, Varmora International [capital_structure, page 109], which is a real red flag — promoter leverage funded against listed shares can force sales in a falling market. There are also disclosed criminal proceedings against promoters and the company [litigation, page 437-438], with materiality not assessable from the extracts given.

Key risks it identified

  • Promoter share pledge for a related-entity loan — Pledged promoter shares create forced-selling risk and indicate the promoter group is itself cash-strained, misaligned with new minority shareholders. [capital_structure, page 109]

  • Supplier and input dependence — Dependence on top-10 suppliers for key raw materials in a thin-margin business means input disruption passes straight to profit. [business, page 241]
