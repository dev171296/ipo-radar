Combined with a sharp 78.5% revenue rebound in the latest year as the headline number, the mood at pricing is engineered to be favourable; nothing given shows subscription or anchor data, so this is a pricing-arithmetic view, not a demand view.

On the long term (34). Reported PAT growth of 40.8% CAGR sits on top of operating cash flow that is negative in all three years and worsening in absolute terms (₹-1,807m latest), meaning the profit is being consumed by land and construction inventory faster than collections arrive. Revenue is also down on a two-year compounded basis (−13.6%), so the 'growth story' is a one-year rebound off a collapsed year, and long-term compounding depends entirely on continued access to outside funding.

The Promoter had 37.5m shares pledged and another ~68.8m under non-disposal undertakings, with pledges/NDUs only 'temporarily released' to meet minimum promoter contribution and 5.47m shares re-pledged to a lender afterwards [capital_structure, page 94].
