Groq — in its own words (qwen/qwen3.8-27b)

On listing (65). The subscription of ~11x with a 2-day window suggests moderate retail/institutional interest, but the absence of a 'basis for price' chapter and anchor data makes it impossible to verify if the 258-271 band is fair or seller-friendly. The demand is present but not overwhelming enough to guarantee a significant listing pop without valuation context.

On the long term (55). The business shows strong operational momentum with a 158% increase in order book (from 703.45 to 1586.01 million) and consistent positive free cash flow, indicating a healthy, growing fire protection systems business. However, the lack of visible ROE/ROCE metrics and the inability to verify debt levels from the provided extracts prevent a confident long-term valuation assessment.

On governance (60). The prospectus explicitly states there are no material regulatory findings and no conflicts of interest between promoters and suppliers, which is a positive baseline. However, the reliance on standard related-party disclosure references without specific transaction volumes in the provided text limits the depth of the governance assessment.

On the long term (55). The fundamentals shown are genuinely good: revenue grew from ₹3,729.53 million to ₹4,892.81 million over Fiscal 2024-2026, free cash flow is positive in all three years and rising, and the order book nearly doubled from ₹752.92 million in Fiscal 2024 to ₹1,586.01 million [business, page 212]. But the prospectus gives me no ROE, ROCE, margin trajectory, debt levels or valuation anchor, so the score reflects a decent-looking business that I cannot actually underwrite at ₹271 a share.

The only governance-relevant extracts are boilerplate assertions: no material adverse regulatory findings [litigation, page 366] and a claimed absence of promoter-supplier conflicts of interest [promoters, page 279]. Related-party transactions exist and are disclosed under Accounting Standard 24 [promoters, page 324]; absence of red flags in excerpts is not the same as evidence of good governance.
