On the long term (67). The fundamental evidence is more encouraging than the average speculative IPO: the reported order-book trajectory has strengthened, revenue has expanded across the supplied fiscal periods, and the customer base appears neither narrowly dependent on one buyer nor deteriorating sharply [business, page 212], [business, page 216]. The company also presents itself as a leader and major exporter in its niche, and the supplied cash-flow figures suggest real cash generation rather than purely accounting profits [business, page 219]. A materially higher score is impossible without margins, returns on capital, debt, working-capital detail and the issue-price rationale.

On governance (58). The prospectus discloses no materially adverse regulatory inspection findings and explicitly denies promoter conflicts with raw-material suppliers [litigation, page 366], [promoters, page 279]. These are useful but still seller-side statements, and the extracts provide no independent evidence sufficient for a strong governance score.
