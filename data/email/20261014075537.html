NVIDIA — in its own words (moonshotai/kimi-k3)

On listing (68). Subscription ramped from 0x to ~7.5x by the morning of day 2 of 3, which indicates genuine demand building rather than a thin book; a ~₹5,000 million mainboard issue with positive three-year free cash flow tends to find aftermarket support. However, I have no anchor book, no grey market premium, and no basis-for-price chapter, so I cannot judge whether the ₹258-271 band is priced for the seller; that caps conviction below a strong buy signal.

On the long term (62). The fundamentals shown are genuinely decent: operating cash flow of ₹471.9m, ₹924.9m and ₹983.0m with free cash flow positive and rising across three years, revenue of ~₹4,893m in the latest full year, and an order book that has grown from ~₹703m to ₹1,586m, implying forward revenue visibility [business, page 212]. The company claims to be the largest in its category in India by operating income in Fiscal 2025 and the largest exporter by value [business, page 219], but that is the seller's own claim, I have no ROE, ROCE, margin or leverage data, and the first-quarter revenue of ₹1,090m annualises below the FY2026 level, which could signal a slowdown or just seasonality.

On governance (55). On paper it looks clean: no material adverse findings from SEBI or other regulator inspections [litigation, page 366] and an asserted absence of promoter conflict of interest with suppliers beyond disclosed related-party dealings [promoters, page 279]. But these are the company's own assertions, related-party transactions exist and are only pointed to a disclosure note I was not given, and I could not determine the fresh-issue vs offer-for-sale split, so promoter monetisation intent is unknown.
