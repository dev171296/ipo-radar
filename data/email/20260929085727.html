This is compounded by the fact that the company is priced at a discount to its selected peers, yet the market is not bidding it up, suggesting the 'seller's price' is not being accepted by buyers.

On the long term (20). The business exhibits a classic 'paper profit' profile where high reported PAT coexists with deeply negative operating cash flows in all three years, indicating that revenue is not converting to cash. The extreme volatility in revenue (swinging from ₹24kM to ₹10kM to ₹18kM) suggests a lumpy, project-based model with poor visibility, making long-term compounding difficult to predict.

While the prospectus claims no conflicts of interest, the promoter has a significant portion of shares pledged (37.5 million) and under NDU, which creates a risk of forced selling or loss of control if the stock price drops.
