On listing (85). The subscription data shows a massive acceleration in the final hours, jumping from ~10x to nearly 120x, which indicates strong retail or institutional demand likely driven by the small issue size (₹1,054.97 million). This level of oversubscription typically ensures a listing pop, though the small float may lead to high volatility.

On the long term (65). The business shows improving margins (EBITDA margin rising from 23.84% to 27.66%) and a deleveraging balance sheet (D/E dropping from 0.932 to 0.407), which are positive structural signals. However, revenue growth is modest (4.52% latest year) and receivable days are increasing (94 to 118 days), suggesting potential working capital strain or aggressive credit policies that could erode the quality of earnings over time.

On governance (70). The prospectus states there are no SEBI disciplinary actions against promoters, which is a baseline positive.
